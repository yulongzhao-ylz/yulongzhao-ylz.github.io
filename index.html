Real-Time Early Warning with a Controlled False-Alarm Rate

98.9% caught

Streaming risk scoring · sequence models · threshold calibration

Flags users heading toward failure while they are still working, without crying wolf.

Details

Built a streaming risk-scoring system that reads each user's partial action sequence and decides when to flag users heading toward failure, with alert thresholds calibrated on the deployed trigger rule so false alarms stay within a preset budget (≤5%). Across about 370 configurations (GRU, GRU with attention, temporal CNN, causal Transformer, and leakage-free logistic baselines) on three interactive tasks, the best temporal-CNN model caught 98.9% of failing users at precision ≥0.98, with false alarms at or below 4% on every task.

Time-Aware Modeling of User Event Sequences

96.1% accuracy

Functional regression · adaptive group LASSO · interpretable prediction

Finds which actions matter, and whether their effect depends on when they happen.

Details

Developed an interpretable model for timestamped event logs that tests, for each action, whether its effect on the outcome is absent, constant, or depends on when it happens. On 19,089 events from 2,668 users, it raised cross-validated accuracy from 93.5% to 96.1% and halved log loss (0.237 to 0.118) compared with a count-feature baseline, winning on all 10 folds. The gain comes from actions whose effect flips sign with timing, such as early exploration versus a final choice. See the interactive curve above.

Latent User-Segment and Hidden-State Modeling

3.7× faster

Hidden Markov models · EM · sparse model selection · HPC

Recovers the hidden traits and states behind how users move through a multi-app task.

Details

Designed sparse latent-variable sequence models that combine persistent binary user traits with evolving hidden states, identifying which traits drive action choice, interface choice, and state transitions for 1,340 users of a multi-app task. Implemented a custom forward–backward EM in Python with model selection over up to 64 structures, ran 580+ HPC jobs, and sped up the EM core by up to 3.7× with NumPy/Numba while keeping results bitwise identical.

Time-Series Forecasting and Regime-Shift Detection

18/18 wins

Walk-forward forecasting · surrogate testing · IMSI interdisciplinary cluster

Forecasts biological change from environmental records and tests where regimes shift.

Details

Built a walk-forward forecasting pipeline (RBF-SVR, time-ordered tuning, no look-ahead) that beat the historical-mean baseline in 18 of 18 settings, with 38% lower mean squared error on average. Developed a regime-shift detector across 28 environmental series pairs, tested against 4,999 autocorrelation-preserving surrogates with Holm correction, and stress-tested findings under 49 data perturbations.
